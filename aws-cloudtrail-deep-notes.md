# AWS CloudTrail (Deep Dive)

## What is AWS CloudTrail?

CloudTrail keeps an eye on every activity in the cloud. It checks who did what, like launching EC2 or deleting an S3 bucket. It also records the date and time, and keeps a record of everything.

## Why it matters for security:

- Provides proof of who did what
- Helps investigate incidents
- Records date and time of every action

## One sentence to remember:

CloudTrail records every action in your AWS account — like a security camera for your cloud.
