# IaaS (Infrastructure as a Service)

## What is IaaS?

IaaS is a cloud model where we control everything inside, like apps, data, and services. All physical parts (servers, hardware) are controlled by the cloud provider.

## Examples:

- EC2 (virtual servers)
- S3 (storage)

## One sentence to remember:

IaaS gives you virtual hardware, and you manage everything on top of it.
