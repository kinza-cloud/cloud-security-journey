# AWS Artifact – Theory

## What is AWS Artifact?

AWS Artifact is a service that gives you access to AWS compliance reports and agreements. It helps you prove that AWS meets security and compliance standards.

## What Does Artifact Provide?

| Document Type | What It Shows |
|---------------|---------------|
| SOC Reports | Security and availability controls |
| ISO Certificates | International security standards |
| PCI Reports | Payment card industry compliance |
| HIPAA Agreements | Healthcare compliance |
| GDPR Agreements | Data protection compliance |

## Why is Artifact Important?

- Helps with audits
- Proves AWS compliance
- Free to download reports
- Supports legal and regulatory needs

## One Sentence to Remember

> AWS Artifact gives you the official documents that prove AWS is secure and compliant.
