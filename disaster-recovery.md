# Disaster Recovery (DR)

Disaster Recovery means: if our data is lost, there are multiple ways to recover it.

## How it works:
- If a hacker deletes our data, it is not permanently deleted
- We have backups
- We can recover our data

## Why it matters:
- Users may see the issue, but our reputation stays good
- Data can be restored from backup

## High Availability vs Disaster Recovery:
- HA = keeps app running during failure
- DR = recovers data AFTER a disaster

## One sentence to remember
Disaster Recovery is your plan to restore data and services after a disaster.
