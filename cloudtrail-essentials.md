# AWS CloudTrail – Essentials

## What is CloudTrail?

CloudTrail records every action in your AWS account — like a security camera for your cloud.

## Why it matters:

- You can see who did what and when
- If something goes wrong, you have proof
- Important for AWS exams

## Types of events CloudTrail records:

1. **Management Events** – Actions that change resources
2. **Data Events** – Actions on data (like reading a file)
3. **Insight Events** – Unusual activity

## One sentence to remember:

CloudTrail is like a security camera for your AWS account.
